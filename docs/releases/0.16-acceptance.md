## Limitations and 0.17 blockers

- Volt rows still not V: `accordion` P, `sidebar` P, `autofill` C, `form-field` C, `input-otp` C, `file-upload` C. They are not part of 0.16 and are unchanged.
- WebKit (default settings) does not Tab onto buttons; the focus trap only wraps at its boundaries, so the drawer Tab-trap test uses a last tabbable input. A fully manual Tab trap is not implemented.
- Drawer side is fixed per `open()`; no swipe gestures. Splitter units are percentages; two panels only. Submenu templates must be declared inside the parent menu.
- Full Angular 21/22 SSR/hydration consumer matrix, bundle/tree-shaking checks and the home-route flake are 0.17 work.
