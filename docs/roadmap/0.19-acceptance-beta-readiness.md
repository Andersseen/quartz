# 0.19 — Acceptance and pre-beta stabilization

Status: **engineering implemented on `feat/quartz-0.19-acceptance-beta-readiness`; the minor is NOT accepted or released, and the API is NOT frozen.**
Record: [`docs/releases/0.19-acceptance.md`](../releases/0.19-acceptance.md).

## Why this exists

0.19 is **not** a new component family and is not part of the original 0.10–0.18 plan (parity families 0.10–0.16, consumers 0.17, release and freeze 0.18).
It is an additional acceptance and stabilization minor: 0.17 and 0.18 were implemented and merged (PRs #50, #51) but neither was accepted or published,
the manual assistive-technology gate and the genuine-Autofill gate were open, one Firefox failure had no root cause, and the new publication pipeline had never
published. 1.0.0-beta.N cannot start until those are closed or explicitly waived by the maintainer.

## Non-goals

No new components, no Autofill re-implementation, no pipeline replacement, no Volt UI or Doqs changes, no invented screen-reader or consumer results, no maintainer
waiver or freeze on the maintainer's behalf, no publication of a beta, RC or 1.0.

## Exit

The engineering PR may merge with external gates pending. The minor is accepted only when the maintainer records it; beta readiness is decided in the acceptance record.
